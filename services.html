Services

Every surface, inside and out.

If it can be painted, stained, or refinished, it's likely something we handle regularly — residential or commercial.

Interior Painting

Interior Painting

Walls, ceilings, doors, trim, and built-ins. We protect your floors and furniture, cut clean lines by hand, and match sheen to how the room actually gets used — eggshell in the living room, something more washable in a kitchen or a kid's bathroom.

Exterior Painting

Exterior Painting

Siding, fascia, soffits, eaves, and trim. Okanagan summers and winters are hard on a paint job, so we scrape, sand, and prime properly before a single top coat goes on — that's the part that decides whether it lasts five years or fifteen.

Cabinet & Trim Refinishing

Cabinet & Trim Refinishing

A full cabinet respray or a simple trim refresh, sprayed or brushed depending on the finish you want. A good way to change the feel of a kitchen without a full renovation budget.

Deck & Fence Staining

Cleaning, sanding, and staining for decks, fences, and runnerboards — built to handle foot traffic and direct sun without peeling or graying out early.

Surface Prep & Pressure Washing

Sometimes the job is just getting a surface ready — pressure washing siding and decks, patching drywall, or sanding down old paint before a repaint.

Not sure which category your project falls into?

Most quotes end up being a mix of a few of these. Tell us what you're picturing and we'll figure out the scope together.
