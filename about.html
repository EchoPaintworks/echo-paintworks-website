About

Why "Echo"

The name comes from a place, not a marketing exercise.

Echo Paintworks is named after Echo Lake — a spot our founder, Evan Fredin, spent a lot of evenings at with his now-wife back when they were dating, sitting by the fire and talking about what came next.

When it came time to name a painting company, "Echo" fit for a simple reason: what you put in comes back to you. Put in careful prep, honest pricing, and materials that actually hold up, and that's what echoes back — a finish that still looks right years later, and customers who call again for the next room, or the next building.

Echo Paintworks is based in Vernon and takes on residential and commercial work across the Okanagan Valley.
